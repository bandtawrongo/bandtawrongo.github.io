The band

Live music for the nights people remember

Tawrongo is an eight-piece band from Orlando. We play the songs our audiences grew up with, from Rabindrasangeet and modern Bengali favourites to Bollywood classics, Sufi anthems and South Indian chartbusters, arranged for a full live band.

i.

A real band, every instrument live

Two vocalists, lead and rhythm guitar, bass, piano, drums, tabla and hand percussion.

ii.

A set shaped around your crowd

Devotional openers, singalongs, dance numbers and instrumentals, sequenced to fit your event.

iii.

Concert-ready

Full-length stage shows with our own setlist, changeovers and production planning.
